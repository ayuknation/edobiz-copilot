# EdoBiz Copilot 🇳🇬

### Talk your business. EdoBiz handles the numbers.

EdoBiz Copilot is an AI-powered business assistant designed to help Nigerian small businesses record transactions, understand their finances, and make better business decisions using natural language.

The goal is simple: **make business intelligence accessible to small business owners without requiring them to understand accounting software or Excel.**

---

## 💡 The Problem

Many small businesses in Nigeria operate without proper digital financial records.

Business owners often know how much they sell, but may struggle to answer questions like:

- How much profit did I make this month?
- Which product makes me the most money?
- How much did I spend on transportation?
- Is my business growing?
- Can I afford to restock?
- How healthy is my cash flow?

EdoBiz Copilot aims to turn everyday business conversations into useful financial information.

---

## 🚀 Our Solution

EdoBiz allows business owners to record business activities using **natural language**.

For example:

> "I sell 5 bags of rice today for 425k. I spend 9k for transport."

EdoBiz can interpret the information and structure it as:

| Transaction | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Sales | ₦425,000 |
| Transport expense | ₦9,000 |
| Net contribution | ₦416,000 |

The user can then ask:

> "How my business dey do this month?"

And receive a simple business performance summary.

---

## 🗣️ Nigerian Pidgin Support

A major feature of EdoBiz Copilot is the ability to interact using **Nigerian English and Pidgin**.

Examples:

> "I buy 10 cartons of drinks for 180k."

> "How much I make this week?"

> "Which product dey give me more profit?"

The goal is to allow users to communicate naturally instead of forcing them to learn complicated financial software.

## 🏦 Financial Inclusion

Many micro and small businesses have limited formal financial records.

EdoBiz aims to help businesses build a consistent digital record of their activities.

With user consent, these records could eventually help businesses better demonstrate their financial activity when seeking appropriate formal financial services.

**EdoBiz does not make loan or credit decisions.**
